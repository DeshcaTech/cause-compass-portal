# Community Hub

Home à Home page just with the attached logo . The Donate button that link to donate page. The Support Our Causes button that link to the Fundraising page. The download button for android and apple

*About CCGMs  

- President speech for president message and the board team and the button

- Board page where we can see current team by default, and past teams by clicking 

- Download page to download different documents 

- Assets rent page where members can request the community asset

Membership  Members can pay their membership as individual, student, or family. Included in family membership mum, dad and children up to 21years old. They will receive an email confirming their registration and a unique registration number. Information request for registration are full name, address, phone number, month and year of birth. For additional family membership, select pathner or dependent, allow to add full name, month/year of birth, and phone if it is a partner.

Events  Event page where members are informed about different event, coming events, past events, all events. Also, a monthly calendar that can list events per day. When click on an event, a popup window appears with more information about the event. There is two types of events: CCGMs events and Other events 

Prathers  Partner page to advertise business own by members of the community. When click on a business, a popup window appears with more information about the business.

Gallery  Photo gallery page where photo will be grouped per event. Here, the admin can choose the default open gallery 

Get Involve   

For all the get involve action, always request the Membership Number as optional but recommended

- Fundraising page with different campaign where member can see active campaign and pass campaign, and also give the option for random donation

- Survey page for active and current survey – Become a volunteer page 

- Donate page for random donation with the option for active Fundraising 

-  Refer page to refer someone that might need our support

Contact  Contact page
